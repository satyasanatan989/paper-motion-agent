# Food Code Research Dossier: Digestion & Physiology of Instant Noodles

## 1. Overview & De-sensationalization
Instant noodles are precooked steamed-and-fried wheat noodles primarily composed of refined wheat flour (endosperm starch), vegetable oil (often palm oil), and a seasoning sachet containing sodium chloride, monosodium glutamate (MSG), and spices.

Scientific fact: Digestion of instant noodles follows standard human gastrointestinal physiology. It is not an impermeable wax that stays in the stomach for years, nor is it acute poison. Digestion breaks starch into maltose and glucose, triglycerides into fatty acids, and absorbs sodium in the small intestine.

## 2. Anatomical & Biochemical Stages

### Stage A: Oral Cavity (Mouth)
- **Mastication**: Mechanical shearing breaks noodle strands into a moist bolus.
- **Salivary Amylase ($\alpha$-amylase)**: Initiates hydrolysis of $\alpha$-(1,4)-glycosidic bonds in amylose and amylopectin, producing dextrins and maltose.

### Stage B: Gastric Processing (Stomach)
- **Peristaltic Retropulsion**: Smooth muscle contractions (antral milling) mix the bolus with gastric juices (HCl, pepsin).
- **Starch Digestion Pause**: Salivary amylase is inactivated when gastric pH drops below 3.5.
- **Chyme Formation**: Food is reduced to an acidic semi-fluid chyme and metered through the pyloric sphincter into the duodenum at $\approx 2-4\text{ kcal/min}$.

### Stage C: Duodenum & Small Intestine
- **Pancreatic Neutralization & Enzymes**: Secretin and CCK trigger bicarbonate (neutralizing pH to 6.5–7.5) and pancreatic $\alpha$-amylase.
- **Brush Border Enzymes**: Maltase and isomaltase cleave disaccharides into absorbable monosaccharide D-glucose.

### Stage D: Vascular Absorption & Metabolism
- **SGLT-1 Transporters**: Glucose is actively transported across apical enterocyte membranes into mesenteric capillaries.
- **Systemic Circulation**: Enters portal vein $\to$ liver $\to$ systemic blood for cellular ATP synthesis (glycolysis) or glycogen storage.

### Stage E: The Sodium Factor
- Sodium content varies dramatically between brands ($800\text{mg} - 1800\text{mg}$ per packet). High dietary sodium draws water into vasculature, transiently impacting blood pressure and osmotic balance.

### Stage F: Nutritional Optimization (The Balanced Bowl)
- Refined noodles lack dietary fiber and essential micronutrients. Adding cruciferous vegetables (bok choy, carrots) restores peristaltic fiber; adding an egg or tofu restores protein balance and blunts postprandial glucose spikes.
